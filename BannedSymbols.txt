// keeps time deterministic and testable via FakeDateProvider.

// Use Guid.CreateVersion7() instead — time-ordered GUIDs are index-friendly (sequential inserts)
// and sort naturally by creation time, unlike random v4 GUIDs from Guid.NewGuid().
M:System.Guid.NewGuid;Use Guid.CreateVersion7() instead of Guid.NewGuid() for time-ordered identifiers.
